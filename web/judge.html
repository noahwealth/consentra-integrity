What to try, and what should happen.

No login. Each visitor gets a private copy of the demo patient, so you can do anything without affecting anyone else. All data is synthetic. Direct links open the screen straight away.

Sign in

Two doors: email magic link (Supabase Auth, PKCE, passwordless) creates a permanent account; "Try the demo" opens an anonymous session that can later be saved to an email in place (same user id, nothing lost).

Demo opens instantly with your own private copy of the demo patient. Email sends a one-time link.
1

Passport — /#/passport

Noah Dixon, three providers (UK + US), 32 records shown per provider copy — never merged.

3 sources, 32 records, "Not verified" badge on the passport card, Normalization strip (RxNorm · LOINC · UCUM).
2

Verify — /#/scan → "Verify my health records"

Cross-provider rules run in Postgres; Sodabase is asked (MCP, from a Supabase Edge Function) for its trust verdict on each provider feed and three patient-scoped rules. In the product these appear as "Source health" — patients never see vendor names.

Six findings for the demo patient (penicillin allergy missing at one provider — critical; lisinopril 10 vs 20 mg; stale omeprazole; asthma status conflict; provider without identifier; duplicate ibuprofen). "Source health" chips per feed: Up to date / Check recommended / Needs attention / Checking.
3

Resolve — /#/resolve

Each finding side by side with the provider that holds it, the timestamp, and the dose in canonical units. Flag it with a note; attach any image as evidence (Supabase Storage, private bucket).

"Flagged for provider" tag appears; evidence row appears with a View button that opens a signed URL.
4

Share — /#/share

Create a 24-hour share for "Dr. Rivera" with Medications + Allergies. Open the clinician link in a new tab.

Clinician page shows only those categories; flagged rows say "In question"; your note and evidence link are listed; HbA1c 48 mmol/mol shows "US: 6.5 %" beside it.
5

Request access (clinician) → consent sheet (patient)

On the clinician page, "Request access" for Conditions + Lab Results. Back on /#/passport a consent sheet appears; drop one scope; Allow.

Clinician page updates to "Allowed: …" with an Open link; the patient's Activity tab lists receipts "Access requested" and "Request allowed"; "Verify receipts" reports all verified (sha256 hash chain; Export gives a FHIR R4 AuditEvent).
6

Revoke

On /#/share, Revoke the share and watch the clinician tab.

Clinician tab reads "Access ended" within ~5 s; the evidence link now returns 403; a "Access revoked" receipt is written.
